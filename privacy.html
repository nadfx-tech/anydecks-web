Privacy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

anydecks lets you play back-to-back with other DJs, wherever you are.

This page explains what data anydecks processes, why, and how long we keep it.

1. Your account

When you create an account, we store your DJ name, a key generated on your computer, your friends and pending friend requests, and the date you joined.

You can also add a bio, links and the styles you play.

We use this to run your account and to connect you with other DJs.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2. Signing in

With Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your Google user ID. That's how we recognise your account on every computer you use.

We request nothing but your identity (OpenID). We never receive your email address, your contacts or any other data from your Google account.

Google may process data of its own during sign-in. Google's privacy policy applies to that.

With an email code

You can also sign in with a six-digit code that we send to your email address.

We don't store your email address – only a SHA-256 hash of it, so we can recognise your account when you come back.

A hash isn't fully anonymous: anyone who already knows an address can compute its hash and compare.

The email is sent by Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France). For this, your email address and the code are passed to Brevo.

The code expires after ten minutes and is only ever kept in memory.

Your computer's pass

After you sign in, your computer receives a pass signed by our server, valid for 30 days. It lets your partner's app check that you're signed in.

The pass is stored only on your computer.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3. Friends, lobby and board

Invitations

We store each invitation code, who created it and who used it.

Lobby

If you turn on the lobby, other signed-in DJs can see your DJ name while you're online and send you a friend request.

Turn it off and you disappear from the lobby straight away.

Board

On the board, you can post when you'd like to play, with a short message.

Other signed-in DJs see your posts together with your DJ name.

Each post is deleted automatically once its time has passed. You can remove it at any time.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4. Connections and server logs

To connect you with your partner, our server needs your IP address and network port. It keeps them in memory only, and only while you're online.

To keep the service running smoothly, the server logs how many people are online once a minute, plus general events such as sign-ins and new invitations.

The log contains no DJ names, IP addresses or email addresses. Once it reaches about 1 MB, the oldest entries are overwritten.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for connecting you, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for keeping the service secure and reliable.

5. Your music stays yours

End-to-end encrypted

Your tracks and your mix go straight to your partner, end-to-end encrypted.

If a direct connection isn't possible, the encrypted data is relayed through our server. The server can't decrypt it and doesn't store it.

Shared tracks

Tracks you share are kept in your partner's memory.

If they don't fit, the rest is cached on your partner's drive – encrypted, with a key that only exists while the app is running.

Everything is deleted when the app closes, or one minute after you leave the session.

Recordings and play history

Your recordings and play history are stored only on your computer.

Your library

anydecks can read your rekordbox, Traktor, Serato, VirtualDJ or djay Pro library.

This happens entirely on your computer. Your library is never uploaded.

6. Cookies

On get.anydecks.app, we use a single essential cookie to keep you signed in.

It expires when you close your browser, after twelve hours at most.

If you tick "Stay signed in", it stays valid for up to 30 days after your last visit.

Our server stores only a fingerprint of the cookie. It's used to keep you signed in – never for advertising or tracking.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and § 25(2) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act).

7. Service providers

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

To run anydecks, we use:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) to send sign-in codes.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs our servers in Germany.

Where a provider processes personal data on our behalf, a data processing agreement is in place.

Possible transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area and the safeguards involved still need to be fully verified.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it, or as long as the law requires.

  • Account and profile: as long as your account exists. How soon after closing an account it is deleted is still to be decided.
  • Email codes: ten minutes, in memory only.
  • Your computer's pass: 30 days.
  • Connection data: only while you're online.
  • Lobby: until you turn it off or go offline.
  • Board: until the time of your post has passed or you remove it.
  • Server log: overwritten once it reaches about 1 MB.
  • Shared tracks: deleted when the app closes, or one minute after you leave the session.
  • Website sign-in: twelve hours at most, or up to 30 days after your last visit with "Stay signed in".

9. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account and your personal data at any time. Just email privacy@anydecks.app.

We'll delete everything, unless the law requires us to keep it.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data (Art. 15),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data erased (Art. 17),
  • restrict processing (Art. 18),
  • take your data with you (Art. 20),
  • object to certain processing (Art. 21).

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, with effect for the future.

You can also complain to a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

11. No ads. No tracking.

We don't use your data for personalised advertising, we don't build advertising profiles, and we don't sell user data.

Our servers are in Germany.
