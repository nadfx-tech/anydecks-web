Privacy

As of 11 November 2026 · Deutsch

anydecks is a desktop app for DJs who play back to back from different places. It is in a private beta. This page explains what data the app and its meeting server handle, and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your DJ name, a key generated on your computer (so the server recognises it), your friends and open friend requests, the date the account was made, and – if you fill them in – bio, links and styles.
  • Sign-in with Google or Facebook: only the provider's anonymous user number (for example "google:1234…"), so you get the same account on another computer. anydecks asks only for your identity (openid / public_profile). We do not receive or store your e-mail address, contacts or anything else from your Google or Facebook account.
  • Invitations: the invitation code, who made it and who used it.

What we use briefly

  • Internet address (IP) and port of your computer while the app is open, so you and your partner can find each other. It is kept in memory only while you are online.
  • A small server log: once a minute, which DJ names are online and whether a connection uses the server as a relay. It overwrites itself after about 1 MB.

Music, sound and files

Your mix and your tracks go directly from your computer to your partner's, encrypted end to end. If a direct connection is not possible, the encrypted data passes through our server, which cannot read it and does not store it. Tracks shared in Booth are deleted on your partner's computer when the app closes. Recordings are saved only on your own computer.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data. The app uses the microphone input only to hear the channel your mixer is connected to; nothing is recorded unless you press Record.

Where the data is

The meeting server runs in Germany. Accounts are kept as long as they exist; delete yours by asking us, and everything listed above is removed.

Your rights

You can ask what we store about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to a data protection authority (GDPR, Art. 15–21 and 77).

Datenschutz

anydecks ist eine Desktop-App für DJs, die von verschiedenen Orten aus Back-to-Back auflegen (geschlossene Beta).

Gespeichert

DJ-Name, ein auf Deinem Rechner erzeugter Schlüssel, Freunde und offene Anfragen, Anlegedatum, freiwillig Bio, Links und Styles. Bei Anmeldung mit Google oder Facebook nur die anonyme Nutzernummer des Anbieters – keine E-Mail-Adresse, keine Kontakte. Einladungen: Code, wer eingeladen hat, wer ihn genutzt hat.

Kurzzeitig verarbeitet

IP-Adresse und Port, solange die App online ist (nur im Arbeitsspeicher), damit Ihr Euch findet. Ein kleines Protokoll: einmal pro Minute, welche DJ-Namen online sind; es überschreibt sich nach etwa 1 MB.

Musik und Dateien

Mix und Tracks gehen Ende-zu-Ende-verschlüsselt direkt zum Partner, notfalls verschlüsselt über unseren Server, der sie weder lesen noch speichern kann. Geteilte Tracks werden beim Beenden gelöscht, Aufnahmen bleiben nur auf Deinem Rechner.

Nicht

Keine Werbung, kein Tracking, keine Weitergabe. Der Server steht in Deutschland. Auskunft, Berichtigung, Löschung und Beschwerde bei einer Aufsichtsbehörde nach DSGVO Art. 15–21 und 77. Kontakt: privacy@anydecks.app.
